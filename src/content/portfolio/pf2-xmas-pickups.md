A few holiday themed item pickups that was shipped with [Pre-Fortress 2's Cold Snap Update](https://prefortress.com/updates/0.7.4/). I designed, modelled, and textured the item pickups. The icon on the grenade pack was created by [HypnOS 1999](https://bsky.app/profile/hypnos1999.bsky.social).
