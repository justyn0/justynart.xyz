A end of round set piece for CTF Cloister that was shipped with [Pre-Fortress 2's Cold Snap Update](https://prefortress.com/updates/0.7.4/). I modelled, textured, rigged, and created the particles for the laser. Animation and sound effects was created by [Farlander](https://x.com/Farlander41). The laser design and implementation of the end of round sequence was by gamingcam2008.
